Question: How do I change my delivery address?
Answer: Call 1800-123-4567 (toll free, 9 AM to 8 PM, Mon-Sat) or email support@zaldi.in before your order is dispatched.

Question: Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Answer: Yes. COD is available for orders up to Rs. 15,000 with a Rs. 49 COD fee.

Question: Can I pay using UPI?
Answer: Yes. We accept Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM UPI, as well as cards, net banking and wallets via Razorpay.

Question: How long does delivery take?
Answer: 2-4 business days in metro cities and 4-7 business days elsewhere in India. Remote areas can take 8-12 days.

Question: What is your return policy?
Answer: 7 days from delivery for unused products with tags. Free reverse pickup at most pincodes.

Question: Can I get a GST invoice for my business?
Answer: Yes. Enter your GSTIN at checkout and the invoice will be issued in your company name.

Question: Which sarees are good for weddings?
Answer: Banarasi, Kanjivaram and Mysore silk sarees are popular for weddings.

Question: How do I wash a chikankari kurta?
Answer: Hand-wash gently in cold water with mild detergent and dry in shade. Do not wring or bleach.
