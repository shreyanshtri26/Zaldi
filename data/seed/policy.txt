## SHIPPING & DELIVERY
Zaldi delivers to over 19,000 pincodes across India through Delhivery, Blue Dart and India Post.
Standard delivery takes 4-7 business days. Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune) usually receive orders in 2-4 business days.
Express delivery (1-2 business days in metro cities) is available for Rs. 149.
Shipping is free on orders above Rs. 999. For orders below Rs. 999 a flat shipping fee of Rs. 79 applies.
Orders are dispatched within 24-48 hours of confirmation. A tracking link is sent by SMS, WhatsApp and email.
We currently deliver only within India. Remote locations such as the North-East, Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep may take 8-12 business days.

## PAYMENT OPTIONS
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, wallets and EMI on cards for orders above Rs. 3,000.
Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to Rs. 15,000 with a COD fee of Rs. 49.
All online payments are processed securely through Razorpay.
No-cost EMI for 3 and 6 months is available on selected bank cards for orders above Rs. 5,000.

## RETURNS & EXCHANGES
Products can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery if they are unused, unwashed and have original tags.
Free reverse pickup is available at most pincodes. If pickup is not available, you can ship the product back and we will reimburse up to Rs. 100.
Sarees with fall/pico done, customised or stitched items, worn jewellery, innerwear and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.
Exchanges for size are free and subject to stock availability.

## REFUNDS
Refunds for prepaid orders are credited to the original payment source (UPI, card or bank account) within 5-7 business days after the returned product passes quality check.
For COD orders, refunds are sent via bank transfer or UPI. You will be asked for your UPI ID or bank details.
You may also choose Zaldi Wallet credit, which is added instantly after quality check.

## SIZE & FIT GUIDE
Women's tops and kurtas are available in XS to XXL. Men's kurtas are in S to XXL; sherwanis, jackets and suits use chest size in inches (38 to 46).
Footwear sizes follow Indian (UK/India) sizing. Kidswear is listed by age group.
Each product page has a size chart in inches. If you are between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger one for ethnic wear.

## GST & INVOICES
All prices are inclusive of GST. A GST invoice is emailed with every order. You can add your business GSTIN at checkout to receive a GST invoice in your company name.
Clothing priced up to Rs. 1,000 attracts 5% GST and above Rs. 1,000 attracts 12% GST as per prevailing rates.

## CARE INSTRUCTIONS
Silk sarees and sherwanis should be dry-cleaned only. Chikankari and embroidered kurtas should be hand-washed gently in cold water with mild detergent and dried in shade.
Block-printed and bandhani cotton may release colour in the first wash; wash separately.
Store sarees folded in muslin cloth and refold every few months to avoid cuts along the folds.
