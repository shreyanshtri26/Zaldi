# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Zaldi to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or language. We pledge to make
taking part a harassment-free experience.

## What we expect

-   Be kind and respectful. Assume good faith; ask before you assume.
-   Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
-   Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
-   Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## What is not acceptable

-   Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
-   Publishing someone else's private information.
-   Sexualised language or attention, trolling, or sustained disruption.
-   Anything that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [shreyanshtri26](https://github.com/shreyanshtri26), through GitHub. Reports are handled
promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and may warn or ban people who do.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
